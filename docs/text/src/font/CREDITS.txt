The typeface data in this folder is derived from the Hershey Fonts.

The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working
at the U. S. National Bureau of Standards.

The format of the font data in the distribution these files were converted
from was originally created by:

    James Hurt
    Cognition, Inc.
    900 Technology Park Drive
    Billerica, MA 01821

The Hershey distribution states that the fonts may be used by anyone for any
purpose, commercial or otherwise, provided the two acknowledgements above are
distributed with the font data, and provided the data is not redistributed in
the specific format published by the U.S. NTIS. The JSON here is our own
format, converted by tools/convert-jhf.mjs, so that condition is met.

Faces included, and the Hershey originals they come from:

    sans          futural     (Futura Light / simplex sans)
    sans-bold     futuram     (Futura Medium)
    serif         rowmans     (Roman Simplex)
    serif-bold    rowmand     (Roman Duplex)
    serif-italic  timesi      (Times Italic)
    script        scripts     (Script Simplex)
    script-bold   scriptc     (Script Complex)
    gothic        gothiceng   (Gothic English)

Conversion notes: Hershey hangs each glyph off the character's centre rather
than the baseline, so the converter shifts every face down onto its own
baseline (taken from a flat-bottomed capital) and records the resulting cap
height. Coordinates are flipped to y-up.
