Pembatalan & Bayaran Balik

Dikemas kini: 20 Oktober 2026

Barang SifuLaser dibuat khas ikut spesifikasi anda (saiz, bahan, reka bentuk). Kerana itu pembatalan dan bayaran balik mengikut peraturan berikut.

1. Sebelum kerja bermula

Anda boleh membatalkan tempahan dan menerima bayaran balik penuh selagi kerja belum bermula. Hubungi kami secepat mungkin selepas bayaran.

2. Selepas kerja bermula

Tempahan tidak boleh dibatalkan dan tiada bayaran balik, kerana barang dibuat khas dan tidak boleh dijual kepada orang lain.

3. Barang rosak atau tidak ikut tempahan

Sila semak barang semasa mengambil. Jika barang rosak atau tidak mengikut spesifikasi yang dibayar, kami akan membaikinya atau membuat semula tanpa caj. Jika itu tidak dapat dilakukan, anda menerima bayaran balik penuh.

4. Barang tidak diambil

Barang disimpan selama 30 hari dari tarikh kami maklumkan ia siap. Selepas itu, barang dianggap ditinggalkan dan tiada bayaran balik.

5. Akses digital

Tiada bayaran balik untuk pakej Akses Penuh selepas akses dibuka.

6. Cara memohon

Hubungi kami melalui WhatsApp atau emel dengan nombor tempahan anda. Bayaran balik yang diluluskan dikembalikan ke kaedah bayaran asal dalam 14 hari bekerja.

Cancellation & Refund

Last updated: 20 October 2026

SifuLaser items are made specially to your specification (size, material, design). For that reason cancellations and refunds follow these rules.

1. Before work starts

You may cancel your order for a full refund as long as work has not started. Contact us as soon as possible after paying.

2. After work starts

The order cannot be cancelled and is not refundable, because the item is made specially and cannot be sold to anyone else.

3. Damaged or incorrect items

Please inspect your item when you collect it. If it is damaged or does not match the specification you paid for, we will repair or remake it free of charge. If that is not possible, you receive a full refund.

4. Uncollected items

Items are kept for 30 days from the date we tell you they are ready. After that they are treated as abandoned and are not refundable.

5. Digital access

Full Access plans are not refundable once access has been opened.

6. How to request

Contact us by WhatsApp or email with your order number. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 14 working days.
