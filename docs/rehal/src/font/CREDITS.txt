Typefaces in this folder come from two sources.


1. Sans and Script - derived from the Hershey Fonts
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working
at the U. S. National Bureau of Standards.

The format of the font data in the distribution these were converted from was
originally created by:

    James Hurt
    Cognition, Inc.
    900 Technology Park Drive
    Billerica, MA 01821

The Hershey distribution states that the fonts may be used by anyone for any
purpose, commercial or otherwise, provided the two acknowledgements above are
distributed with the font data, and provided the data is not redistributed in
the specific format published by the U.S. NTIS. The JSON here is our own
format, converted by tools/convert-jhf.mjs, so that condition is met.

    sans    <- futural   (Futura Light, simplex sans)
    script  <- scripts   (Script Simplex)

Only these two are shipped, and that is not an oversight. Nearly every other
Hershey face is a "duplex", "complex" or "triplex" cut, which gets its weight
by drawing two or three parallel lines - an outline font in all but name, and
useless for single-line engraving. Of the four faces that are genuinely one
stroke, futural and rowmans share 87 of their 95 glyphs, and scripts and
cursive share every letter A-Z and a-z. So the distribution contains exactly
two distinct single-stroke letterforms, and both are here.

Conversion note: Hershey hangs each glyph off the character's centre rather
than the baseline, so the converter shifts every face down onto its own
baseline (taken from a flat-bottomed capital) and records the resulting cap
height. Coordinates are flipped to y-up.


2. Zahid Laser, Zahid Cursive and Sham - original designs
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Built by tools/build-fonts.mjs. Every coordinate is generated from the
construction rules stated in that file:

    zahid   narrow squared techno ("Zahid Laser"); stems on a fixed grid, every curve
            replaced by a 3-unit chamfer, all terminals cut flat. Marked
            "straight" so the renderer never curve-fits it - the chamfers
            are the design.

    sham    wide geometric deco capitals ("Sham"); the O is a true circle of radius
            cap/2, straight lines meet it without transition, apexes are
            points, and lowercase repeats the capitals.

    zahid-cursive
            joined-up handwriting ("Zahid Cursive"). Every lowercase letter
            enters at (0, 3) and leaves at (advance, 3), so consecutive
            letters hand the line to each other and the writing genuinely
            connects - which the Hershey script face never did, its letters
            sit apart. The 12-degree slant is built into the outlines because
            the connections are geometrically tied to it. Capitals are wider,
            simpler and unconnected, which is the usual pairing for a script.

The genres were inspired by the "Square" and "Multicolore" faces on
templatemaker.nl. Nothing is traced or copied from them: their Multicolore is
used under a permission granted specifically to that site, and their Square is
an adaptation by Shriinivas of Square Grotesk. These are our own constructions
in the same two styles, and are ours to license as we wish.
