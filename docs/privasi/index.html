Dasar Privasi

Dikemas kini: 20 Oktober 2026

Dasar ini menerangkan cara SYNAPSIS ENTERPRISE, SSM 201003171487 (SA0163793-U), pengendali sifulaser.com, mengendalikan maklumat anda selaras dengan Akta Perlindungan Data Peribadi 2010 (Malaysia).

1. Maklumat yang kami kumpul

  • Akaun: nama dan alamat emel akaun Google anda bila anda daftar masuk.
  • Reka bentuk tersimpan: tetapan reka bentuk yang anda simpan (saiz, pilihan, nama), bukan fail potong.
  • Tempahan: nama, nombor telefon, butiran barang dan status bayaran.
  • Statistik lawatan: bilangan lawatan tanpa cookie melalui Cloudflare Web Analytics.
  • Dalam pelayar anda: tetapan alat disimpan dalam pelayar anda sendiri (localStorage) dan tidak dihantar kepada kami.

2. Maklumat kad dan bank

Bayaran diproses terus oleh gerbang pembayaran. Kami tidak menerima atau menyimpan nombor kad atau maklumat perbankan anda.

3. Tujuan

Untuk menyediakan akaun dan menyimpan reka bentuk anda, memproses dan menyiapkan tempahan, menghubungi anda tentang tempahan, dan menambah baik laman ini.

4. Perkongsian

Kami tidak menjual maklumat anda. Maklumat hanya dikongsi dengan penyedia servis yang menjalankan laman ini: Google Firebase (akaun dan pangkalan data), Cloudflare (DNS dan statistik), GitHub Pages (hosting), gerbang pembayaran (Bayarcash), penyedia emel untuk notifikasi tempahan, dan WhatsApp bila anda menghubungi kami.

5. Tempoh simpanan

Maklumat disimpan selagi akaun anda aktif, atau selama yang dikehendaki undang-undang untuk rekod tempahan dan cukai.

6. Hak anda

Anda boleh meminta untuk melihat, membetulkan atau memadam maklumat anda dengan menghubungi kami. Reka bentuk tersimpan boleh dipadam sendiri dalam "Reka bentuk saya".

7. Keselamatan

Akses kepada data dihadkan: setiap akaun hanya boleh membaca rekodnya sendiri, dan hanya pentadbir boleh melihat rekod tempahan.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains how SYNAPSIS ENTERPRISE, SSM 201003171487 (SA0163793-U), operator of sifulaser.com, handles your information in line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: your Google account name and email address when you sign in.
  • Saved designs: the settings of designs you save (sizes, options, name), not the cut files.
  • Orders: name, phone number, item details and payment status.
  • Visit statistics: cookie-free visit counts through Cloudflare Web Analytics.
  • In your browser: tool settings are kept in your own browser (localStorage) and are not sent to us.

2. Card and bank details

Payments are processed directly by the payment gateway. We never receive or store your card number or banking details.

3. Purpose

To provide your account and saved designs, to process and complete orders, to contact you about orders, and to improve this website.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your information. It is shared only with the service providers that run this website: Google Firebase (accounts and database), Cloudflare (DNS and statistics), GitHub Pages (hosting), the payment gateway (Bayarcash), an email provider for order notifications, and WhatsApp when you contact us.

5. Retention

Information is kept while your account is active, or as long as the law requires for order and tax records.

6. Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your information by contacting us. You can delete saved designs yourself in "Reka bentuk saya" (My designs).

7. Security

Access to data is restricted: each account can read only its own records, and only administrators can see order records.
