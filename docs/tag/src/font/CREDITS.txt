The single-line face in src/geom/hershey-sans.js
======================================================================

Every word this tool engraves without an outline font - the name on the
front, the "if found" card on the back, and the FRONT / BACK marks in the
export - is set in this face. It must travel with the tool, and this
acknowledgement must travel with it. Do not delete this file.

    sans  <-  futural  (Futura Light, simplex sans)

Conversion note: Hershey hangs each glyph off the character's centre
rather than the baseline, so the converter shifts the face down onto its
own baseline (taken from a flat-bottomed capital) and records the
resulting cap height of 21 units. Coordinates are flipped to y-up.

Why a stroke face at all
----------------------------------------------------------------------

A phone number engraved as a filled outline is a raster job: the head
sweeps the block line by line, and at 3 mm the counters of the digits are
smaller than the spot, so it burns for minutes and comes out a smudge. A
single-line face is one pass down the middle of each stroke - seconds,
and legible at 2.5 mm because there is nothing to fill in. Small text on
a small piece is the case this face was made for.
