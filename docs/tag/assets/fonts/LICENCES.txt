Outline typefaces bundled with the Tag Generator
======================================================================

These are the fonts offered by the Text tool, for the artwork a user
places by hand. They are converted to outlines by opentype.js at export
time, so what leaves in the cut file is geometry, not a font.

All seven are under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1, which permits
use, study, modification and redistribution, including in commercial
work, and permits the embedding of the outlines in a document. The full
licence text is at https://openfontlicense.org/

    Inter-Variable.ttf        Inter            Rasmus Andersson       OFL-1.1
    Roboto-Variable.ttf       Roboto           Christian Robertson    OFL-1.1
    RobotoMono-Variable.ttf   Roboto Mono      Christian Robertson    OFL-1.1
    Oswald-Variable.ttf       Oswald           Vernon Adams           OFL-1.1
    BebasNeue-Regular.ttf     Bebas Neue       Ryoichi Tsunekawa      OFL-1.1
    Lobster-Regular.ttf       Lobster          Pablo Impallari        OFL-1.1
    Pacifico-Regular.ttf      Pacifico         Vernon Adams           OFL-1.1

The single-line face used for the tag's own lettering is not here; it is
embedded in src/geom/hershey-sans.js and credited in src/font/CREDITS.txt.
